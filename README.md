The weirdest job title we could think of: **Professional Stand-In Grandma for Holiday Dinners**.

## The title

> Professional Stand-In Grandma for Holiday Dinners

The joke is the title itself. A stand-in is a real job — someone hired to be somewhere in someone else's place. A grandma is the one person a family cannot hire: she is supposed to be yours, she has known you since you were small, and she asks the questions nobody else is allowed to ask. Put the two together at a holiday dinner and the picture forms on its own: a professional, hired by the hour, arriving with a casserole she did not cook, sitting in grandma's chair, and asking when you are getting married — to a table of strangers who are paying her to do it.

Why not the obvious picks: the field is crowded with apologizers (for robots, geese, vending machines, self-driving cars), mourners (for goldfish, houseplants, strangers), snake milkers and penguin fluffers — and with sheep counters, four of them, including one for insomniacs. The grandma is fresher, still instantly understood, and the humour comes from a real job with one specific twist, not from shock.
